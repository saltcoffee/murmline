# Murmur

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · [Changelog](CHANGELOG.md)

Keep a private timeline inside Obsidian. Write up to 140 characters at a time, as often as you like. Your latest thought appears first, with a thin date line where one day gives way to the next.

## Install

Murmur requires Obsidian **1.8.7 or later** on desktop or mobile.

1. Download `main.js`, `manifest.json`, and `styles.css` from the latest version under [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/saltcoffee/murmur/releases).
2. In your vault, create the folder `.obsidian/plugins/murmur/` and place the three files directly inside it.
3. Restart Obsidian. In **Settings → Community plugins**, enable community plugins if prompted, then turn on **Murmur**.

## Write a thought

Select the feather icon in the ribbon, or open the command palette and choose **Murmur: Capture a thought**. You can assign this command a shortcut in **Settings → Hotkeys**.

Write in the box and choose **Post**. On a keyboard, use **⌘ Enter** on Mac or **Ctrl Enter** on Windows and Linux. Enter alone starts a new line. Spaces and line breaks count toward 140, and a combined emoji such as 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 counts as one character. If you paste more than 140 characters, you can shorten the text in place.

After posting, the box is ready for your next thought. An unfinished draft stays in its tab while you work elsewhere. Post it before closing the tab or restarting Obsidian.

## Revisit or delete

Scroll back through your timeline and choose **Load earlier thoughts** for the next 50 entries. Times and day headings follow your device’s time zone. To remove one thought, open its **More (⋯)** menu, choose **Delete**, review the text, and confirm. Murmur follows your vault’s file deletion preference.

## Your data and language

Each thought is an ordinary Markdown file in your vault’s `Murmur/` folder. You can read it after disabling the plugin. Posting works offline. Your existing vault sync, backup, and sharing settings also apply to these files.

Murmur follows the Obsidian interface language. Chinese language settings display Simplified Chinese. Other language settings display English.
